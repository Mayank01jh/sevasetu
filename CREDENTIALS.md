## 🛡️ Public vs. Protected Access Policy

- **Public Guests (Not Logged In)**:
  - Can browse **Causes & Campaigns**, **Verified NGOs Directory**, **Volunteer Opportunities**, and **Verify QR Codes**.
  - Internal dashboards (Field Lead Tool, NGO Portal, Trust & Admin, and Personal Bill Vault) are strictly protected. Attempting to access protected routes prompts an immediate authentication gate.
  
- **Quick-Fill Demo Feature**:
  - The login modal includes convenient **1-Click Quick Fill** buttons for each role so you can test any access level instantly without manually typing.
