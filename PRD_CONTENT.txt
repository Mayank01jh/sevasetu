A product and delivery specification for a web and mobile platform connecting donors, volunteers, and verified nonprofit organizations.

Purpose and scope
SevaSetu will help people discover verified NGOs and campaigns, make donations, retrieve transaction records, and participate in verified volunteer activities. NGOs will manage profiles, campaigns, donations, volunteer activities, and impact updates. The platform must make verification status and fund-use information understandable while protecting personal and financial data.
This PRD translates the supplied product notes and launch checklist into functional requirements, quality requirements, user journeys, and phased delivery criteria. Items marked TBD require a product, legal, compliance, or technical decision before the affected feature is built or launched.
Product outcomes
Donors can find causes, give through supported methods, and retrieve accurate transaction and tax documents.
Verified NGOs can manage profiles, campaigns, donation records, volunteer activities, and impact evidence.
Volunteers can register, record attendance, and receive verified service records when approved criteria are met.
Platform operators can review organizations, manage access, investigate issues, and maintain auditable records.

1 Product definition and scope
1.1 Vision
SevaSetu is a transparent, compliant digital bridge connecting donors and volunteers with verified nonprofit organizations. It supports giving and volunteering with clear organization status, transaction records, and evidence of impact.
1.2 In scope
NGO registration, verification review, profiles, and status display.
Campaign discovery, custom-amount donations, and campaign administration.
Payment processing integration, donation records, receipts, Bill IDs, and donor/NGO vaults.
Volunteer discovery, sign-up, session codes, attendance review, hours, slips, and certificates.
Fund allocation and milestone reporting, plus media updates tied to campaigns or impact.
Platform administration, role-based access, auditability, security controls, accessibility, legal disclosures, and public-site SEO.
1.3 Out of scope or not yet defined
Payment gateway vendor, settlement schedule, fees, chargeback policy, or reconciliation process.
Exact NGO eligibility policy, evidence checklist, review service levels, renewal schedule, or appeal process.
Final tax-advice language and rules for when an 80G receipt can be issued or corrected.
Detailed mobile app scope beyond the stated Phase 3 release.
Fundraising limits, self-drive approval, payout model, or beneficiary identity-verification policy.
1.4 Product principles
Show verification status with evidence and last-reviewed context; do not imply government endorsement.
Keep donation, payment, receipt, refund, and tax-document states distinct and traceable.
Collect only personal data required for the requested service or legal obligation.
Give users clear control over consent, accessibility, and account records.
Tie impact claims to an NGO, campaign, reporting period, and supporting evidence.

3.4 Payments, donation records, and receipts
ID
Requirement and acceptance criteria
Priority
PAY-01
Checkout supports UPI, cards, and net banking through an approved provider. Record provider reference, amount, currency, status, timestamps, and idempotency key; SevaSetu does not store raw card credentials.
Must
PAY-02
Collect donor identity/PAN when needed for the requested tax benefit or statutory reporting. Explain purpose and optionality where permitted; validate clearly; restrict and audit PAN access.
Must
PAY-03
Create a unique immutable Bill ID for every donation transaction entering the recorded payment lifecycle. Link donor, NGO/campaign, amount, provider reference, and state.
Must
PAY-04
Track pending, successful, failed, refunded, and partially refunded states. Authenticate callbacks, ignore duplicates safely, and show current status to donors.
Must
PAY-05
For qualifying successful donations to eligible NGOs, generate PDF receipt with donor name, PAN as required, receipt ID, NGO tax details, amount/date, and QR verification. Issue only when eligibility and records are verified; QR reveals minimal data.
Must
PAY-06
Provide searchable donor/NGO Bill Vault with receipt downloads. Enforce record-level access; filter by date/status/Bill ID; log downloads.
Must
PAY-07
Support correction, cancellation, refund, and receipt reissue without deleting originals; revised document references superseded receipt. Eligibility/SLA TBD.
Should
PAY-08
Provide controlled NGO reporting data to support applicable donation statements, including Form 10BD readiness. Reconcile fields to transactions/receipts; filing responsibility and format need sign-off.
Should

4 Core user journeys
4.1 Donation
Browse NGOs/campaigns → review purpose and verification → enter custom/suggested amount → provide details/PAN if applicable → confirm consent and eligibility disclosure → pay through provider → receive result and Bill ID → open Bill Vault → download eligible receipt and verify QR.
4.2 Volunteer attendance
Browse opportunity → review requirements → register → arrive → lead opens session and shares code → volunteer checks in and out → lead reviews hours → approved volunteer receives slip/certificate when criteria are met.
4.3 NGO onboarding
Create organization account → submit profile/evidence → platform review and applicable validation → receive decision or information request → respond → publish verified profile → maintain evidence as details expire/change.
4.4 Campaign publication
Authorized user creates draft → adds purpose, dates, goal, disclosures, media → moderation if required → approval or correction request → public discovery → allocation/impact updates → pause or close with final status/report.

6 Non-functional requirements
6.1 Security and privacy
Area
Requirement
Transport/storage
TLS for all traffic. Encrypt sensitive data at rest with AES-256 or approved equivalent; document key management and rotation.
Access
Least privilege and role-based access. Protect administrative actions with stronger authentication; method TBD.
Payments
Use PCI-compliant provider and hosted/tokenized capture; do not store full card data.
Abuse prevention
WAF, DDoS mitigation, bot/rate limiting, brute-force defenses, secure sessions, suspicious-activity monitoring.
Sensitive data
Mask PAN/identity documents by default; restrict access, log reads/downloads, and exclude sensitive data from URLs, analytics, logs, and QR codes.
Auditability
Record sign-ins, verification decisions, campaign changes, payment/receipt states, attendance edits, permission changes, and privileged data access.
Privacy
Publish privacy/cookie/terms/refund policies; minimize data; define purpose/retention; honor applicable requests; document third-party sharing.
Incident response
Define detection, escalation, containment, notification, recovery, and evidence-preservation procedures before launch.
6.2 Reliability, performance, compatibility
Set measurable availability, latency, backup, recovery point, and recovery time objectives before launch (TBD).
Payment callbacks and receipt generation must be retry-safe and idempotent; failures must not create duplicate charges or receipts.
Responsive web supports current major mobile/desktop browsers; exact support matrix TBD.
Validate uploads, scan for malware, enforce size limits, and deliver content safely.
Define and monitor Core Web Vitals/page-load budgets; target thresholds TBD.
Store timestamps consistently and render in user-configured/local time.
6.3 Accessibility and usability
Target WCAG 2.2 AA, subject to accessibility review.
Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, labels, clear validation, and error recovery.
Use meaningful alt text; decorative images add no redundant screen-reader output.
Meet accessible contrast; never use color as the sole status signal.
Capture explicit cookie/form consent where required; optional checkboxes are not preselected.
Use clear action labels and confirm irreversible or financially consequential actions.
6.4 Integrations
Payment gateway for UPI, cards, and net banking; provider and callbacks TBD.
PAN/statutory registry checks: vendor, API, fallback/manual review, and service levels TBD.
Email/SMS/push: provider, message categories, opt-in/out, retries, localization TBD.
QR validation uses a signed or unguessable token and exposes a minimal public record.

8 SEO and visual design
8.1 Search indexing and public pages
Provide sitemap.xml and robots.txt; allow intended public content and keep private/admin routes out of search.
Remove accidental noindex from public pages; use intentional index controls for private/duplicate/low-value pages.
Use canonical URLs and clean semantic slugs for NGO/campaign pages.
Give each indexable page a unique title/meta description, one H1, and sequential heading hierarchy.
Add appropriate schema.org JSON-LD and Open Graph metadata; structured data must match visible content.
Verify production domain in Google Search Console; monitor indexing, sitemap, broken links, and 404s.
Optimize images with WebP/AVIF where supported; link causes, NGOs, campaigns, and updates.
Track Core Web Vitals, mobile usability, HTTPS, crawl errors. Backlink outreach is ongoing marketing, not a software gate.
8.2 Visual system
Use the 60-30-10 color allocation as a guideline: roughly 60 percent dominant background, 30 percent structural/content surfaces, and 10 percent action/highlight accents. Final palette, typography, breakpoints, component library, and brand assets are TBD. Accessibility contrast takes precedence over exact ratios.

10.2 Key risks and mitigations
Risk
Mitigation / decision
Incorrect tax eligibility/receipt
Approve eligibility and templates with compliance; issue only against verified states; preserve versions.
Fraudulent/expired evidence
Document checks, audit decisions, expiry alerts, re-verification, suspension.
Payment mismatch/duplicates
Authenticated callbacks, idempotency, reconciliation, exception queue.
Volunteer code replay/sharing
Short-lived session codes, separate check-in/out, lead approval, anti-abuse policy.
Misleading impact or privacy harm
Require source/period, moderation, consent, takedown route, minimize beneficiary data.
Sensitive data exposure
Restrict/mask PAN, encrypt, audit access, limit retention, inspect logs/analytics.

Which PAN/statutory checks are automated or manual, and what happens when services are unavailable?
What are donation limits, campaign approval rules, self-drive eligibility, beneficiary safeguards, and payout model?
What are retention/deletion, residency, backup, and incident-notification policies?
What service targets apply to uptime, latency, support, recovery, Web Vitals, and code validity?
Which languages, currencies, accessibility evidence, and browser/device support are launch requirements?
Which NGO users can view/export donor data, and what separation-of-duties controls apply?
